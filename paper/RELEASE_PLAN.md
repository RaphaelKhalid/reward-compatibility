# Post-results release plan

Requested by the author on 2026-09-10 while Experiment 002 was running. This is a publication workflow, not an amendment to the frozen experimental protocol.

1. **Complete and export.** Confirm the run is complete, export the released records and regenerate estimates/figures. Retain failures and provenance. Do not publish incomplete results as final or override a scientific/billing pause to obtain a finished paper.
2. **Audit and skeptical review.** Verify task counts, pairing, data reuse, exact scores, uncertainty calculations, model/auditor dependence, costs and the predictive comparison. Complete the human-audit work or explicitly disclose what remains unaudited. Review an anonymized manuscript against its strongest objections, ideally with a fresh context that does not receive promotional framing. Do not call a review by the same assistant that designed/drafted the work genuinely independent or fully blind. It is not venue peer review. Keep a review memo and revision log; label any additional analyses.
3. **Finalize with the human author.** Confirm exact author name/affiliation, scope of claims, responsibility and AI assistance disclosure. Resolve all pending placeholders. Confirm an appropriate distribution license and archive contents; do not infer those binding choices from a general approval to publish.
4. **Zenodo.** Publish the audited manuscript and appropriately licensed reproducibility materials as a clearly labelled preprint/research artifact. Include immutable code/data versions. Record the DOI. Exclude credentials, local tooling and private account information. A DOI is not peer-review acceptance.
5. **Researcher email.** Prepare a short evidence-first email to appropriate verified author(s) of Kaufmann et al., arXiv:2603.30036. Explain the actual contribution, strongest limitation, and link the DOI/code/figure. Do not claim to have solved their general reward-classification problem. Use an authorized sender and verified recipients, without inventing addresses or adding unrelated contacts.
6. **TMLR.** Recheck current policies, prepare the venue template, anonymize manuscript and supplementary materials, and complete the submission using the author's authorized account. Do not link identity-revealing public artifacts from an anonymized submission if venue rules prohibit it. The author must supply/confirm profile, conflicts, licensing and required attestations; do not invent these. Editorial review and acceptance are not guaranteed.

The cloud monitor should notify the main task when results are complete or a blocking issue occurs. It must not publish or send email unattended. No new model calls, changes to the live experiment, or publication actions are authorized by this file itself; authority comes from the user's requests and applicable service permissions.
